## Description

POLDERS is a minimalist sandbox city builder inspired by Townscaper, Terra Nil, and Sandbox crafting games. After a terrible storm, players build dikes, windmills, and sluices to hold back the sea, rebuilding their broken town into a proud 17th-century Dutch trading city. Every building in the game is based on a real Dutch monument or painting by masters such as Vermeer, Van Ruisdael, and Pieck, and the sea will strike mercilessly wherever players forget to check the dikes.

---

## History

Geofront B.V. was founded in 2024 by a team with backgrounds in Architecture, Geodesy, and History. Driven by a desire to share the quintessential Dutch story of facing the sea with the world, the studio began development on POLDERS: A city builder rooted in the real landscapes, buildings, and traditions of the Netherlands. Drawing on extensive conversations with millers, farmers, and cultural institutions, the team set out to create a game that enhances players' real-life connection to Dutch heritage rather than pulling them away from it. POLDERS is scheduled to launch on Steam in Q3 2026.

---

## Features

- **Walk the world you build.** Unlike traditional city builders, players physically walk through a fully explorable voxel world, placing and combining blocks to construct historically authentic buildings.

- **Strategy meets creativity.** Enough depth for experienced gamers, and all the freedom in the world for creative casual players to build the Dutch landscape of their dreams.

- **Fight the sea.** Build and maintain dikes, sluices, and drainage windmills to keep the water at bay. Neglect your defenses, and the sea will flood your settlement without mercy.

- **Living villagers.** Villagers sail barges, operate windmills, haul goods, and patrol dikes: a full automation system that brings the polder to life.

- **Dutch master aesthetics.** Visuals inspired by the paintings of Jacob van Ruisdael and Johannes Vermeer, with hand-crafted models and shaders that capture the characteristic Dutch light, vast skies, and green meadows.

- **Authentic Dutch heritage.** Every structure in the game is modeled after a real Dutch national monument or a painting from the Dutch Golden Age. Players can visit their in-game buildings in real life.
